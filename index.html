No. 06 — The Ritual

Whisked,
never blended.

Three things make a matcha worth 3.800 KD, and none of them are a machine.

01

Sift

Two grams through a fine mesh, every single cup. No clumps, no bitterness, no shortcut.

02

Whisk

80°C water and a bamboo chasen in a W motion until the crema is thick enough to hold a line.

03

Pour

Cold milk first, matcha last, so you get that layer you came here to photograph.

No. 09 — Our Matcha

From Uji,
to your grip cup.

One farm, one cultivar, one grade. We would rather run out than pour a second-harvest cup.

✿

Single origin

First-harvest Okumidori leaf from a family farm in Uji, Kyoto — shaded twenty-one days before picking, which is what makes it sweet instead of grassy.

✿

Ground weekly

Granite-milled in small batches and flown in every few weeks. Matcha starts fading the day it is ground, so we never sit on stock.

✿

Milk bar

Whole, oat, almond or pistachio milk, all included in the price. Sweetness set from zero to three pumps — say the number, we will pour it.

✿

Pink, always

Every cup leaves with the ribbon, the sticker and the strawberry dust if you want it. Being extra is the house style.
